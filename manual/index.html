Add a workspace.

A workspace is one window Castra opens for you.

  • ApplicationAny .exe or shortcut
  • WebsiteOpens in your browser
  • CommandLike Win+R: slack://, calc
  • Open windowsWhat's on screen now, as workspaces

Exactly where you want it.

Click the layout icon on a workspace.

  • Drag or resize the frame right on your display.
  • Or type it — position and size to the pixel.
  • Switch display at the top of the dialog.

Make each one behave.

Click — or double-click a workspace.

  • ZoomEach website at its own zoom.
  • VolumeQuieter or louder, per workspace.
  • App modeA website without the browser toolbar.
  • Auto sign-inPresses “Sign in” once your browser has filled in the saved password.
  • Run as administratorOne UAC prompt when you switch it on, silent after that.
  • Background onlyStarts the app, places no window.
  • TimingHow long Launch all waits before it, and how long Castra waits for its window.
  • Click sequenceClicks for you after the window opens — a login, a tab, a button.
  • NameLeave it empty — Castra names it from the app or address.

Different days, different setups.

Profiles

One set of workspaces per occasion. Switch with the tabs on top; create, rename, duplicate or delete them with next to the tabs.

Displays

  • Click a display or its chip to see only its workspaces. The radar shows all again.
  • Right-click a display to change its colour.
  • Hover a chip — the real display lights up so you know which is which.
  • Plug or unplug a monitor — Castra updates by itself.

The browser, under control.

  • Volume per tab. Castra's volume multiplies the site's own — YouTube at 50% and Castra at 40% plays at 20%.
  • Zoom that sticks, even right after a restart.
  • Auto sign-in for the workspaces where you switch it on.
  • A mixer in the toolbar for every tab that plays sound.

Something off?

Volume or zoom doesn't change

Install the browser extension, then reload the page. The status at the bottom says Extension linked when it's on.

A window opens in the wrong spot

Click the layout icon on the workspace and place it again — drag the frame or type the position.

An app asks for administrator rights every time

Switch on Run as administrator for it: one UAC prompt now, then it starts silently.

Websites open in the wrong browser profile

Settings → Browser profile.

Auto sign-in didn't press the button

Your browser has to fill in the saved password by itself, and the window has to be visible on screen. When it can't sign in, Castra tells you why in a message. It tries once per window — sign in by hand if it didn't work.

Deleted a workspace by mistake

Click Undo in the message that appears right after — it comes back where it was, with all its settings.

Something else

The bug icon, top right: describe it, paste a screenshot, send. It goes straight to the developer.
